**Goal:** Propagate each event's shock along a dated graph of economic links, test whether linked companies follow and how fast, and produce a daily "next in line" report with the evidence behind every link.

**Architecture:** A package `precedent.ripple` on top of the spine. Links live in an append-only, dated store. Propagation is plain linear algebra over the graph as of each session. The first milestone uses only graphs that need no language model (sector membership and return correlation) plus a shuffled placebo, so the method is proven and the bar is set before any link is extracted from text.

**Tech Stack:** As the spine, plus scipy sparse matrices (research extra). Qlib and LightGBM are imported only in the Qlib-facing modules (`ripple_factors.py`, `evaluate.py`) and come from the `research` extra (`uv sync --extra research`); the link store and report modules need only the core dependencies; modules that import scipy (propagation and the statistics) need the `research` extra too. Later, one local embedding model and the parser runtime (both owner-approved).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-predate-design.md`, section 9.

**Depends on:** spine plan Tasks 1 to 4 and 9 to 15 (the old Tasks 1 to 11). Runs in its own worktree alongside the Precedent plan; it touches only `src/precedent/ripple/`, `tests/ripple/`, `stages/3*_ripple_*/` and `references/rungs/m3_ripple_*.yaml`.

## What the research check changed

These were verified on 2026-11-06 and shape the plan:

- The published link effects are monthly. Cohen and Frazzini (2008) report 1.55% a month from customer momentum. A 2023 preprint (Pinchuk, arXiv 2301.11394) finds the spread fell to 0.62% a month for 2005-2018, not significant when value-weighted, and finds no customer momentum at 1 to 10 days; it appears only at 20 and 30 days, equal-weighted. A vendor paper finds no 1-day effect for S&P 500 suppliers.
- So the expected result at 1 and 5 sessions in large caps is no effect. The horizons tested are 1, 5, 20 and 60 sessions, and the report leads with 20.
- Hoberg-Phillips text-based industry links are free to download but keyed by Compustat `gvkey`, with no free crosswalk to ticker or CIK found. Under zero spend they are not used; peers are built from 10-K text instead.
- The accounting rule (ASC 280) requires disclosing that a customer exceeds 10% of revenue, but not the customer's name. Extracted customer-supplier links will be sparse; one published extraction found 149 links for 42 companies.
- Qlib's `HIST` takes a static stock-to-concept matrix with no date axis, and `GATs` takes no graph at all. Neither can use a dated graph unmodified, so the graph enters as features.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in the spine plan's Global Constraints applies.
- Compute-only: no vendor price is displayed or redistributed; outputs are the repo's derived statistics; display rights are an owner gate (thesis section 3).
- **Links come from dated documents.** A model-extracted link without an evidence span found in its source document is rejected. No link is ever created from a model's own knowledge.
- **`graph_asof` is the only way to read links.** A link is visible from the decision cutoff at or after its `valid_from_utc` until `valid_to_utc`. Annual-filing links expire 15 months after their source unless restated (proposed).
- **Adjacency convention:** `A[receiver, source]` is the weight of the source's influence on the receiver. Each receiver's incoming weights are normalized to sum to 1. `ripple = sum over k = 1..hops of alpha^k * A^k * shock`, with `alpha = 0.5` and `hops = 2` (proposed, in `references/ripple_params.json`).
- **Channel names describe where the shock comes from:** `ripple_customer` is the shock arriving from a company's customers, `ripple_supplier` from its suppliers.
- **Shock timing:** a price shock is the source's abnormal close-to-close return on the event's effective session, which is known at that session's cutoff.
- Every graph rung is reported beside the shuffled placebo. A result without its placebo is not reported.

## Review Focus

1. A link whose source filing was accepted after the close: it must not be visible on that session. (Test in Task 1.)
2. A receiver with no incoming links, or a source that is not in the price provider: propagation gives 0 for the first and ignores the second, and both are counted. (Test in Task 3.)
3. A company that is both shocked and linked to itself through a two-hop cycle (A to B to A): no self-propagation at hop 2. (Test in Task 3.)
4. A session with no shocks: the factor panel is empty and the dataset returns zeros. (Test in Task 5.)
5. A restated link: the newer filing extends validity instead of creating a duplicate edge that doubles the weight. (Test in Task 1.)

### Task 3: Propagation

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `build_adjacency(graph: pd.DataFrame, nodes: list[str]) -> tuple[scipy.sparse.csr_matrix, AdjacencyReport]` (rows are receivers, columns are sources, each row normalized to sum to 1 or left all-zero). `AdjacencyReport` has `links_used, links_dropped_unknown_node, receivers_without_links`.

```python
def test_two_hop_chain():
    adjacency, _ = build_adjacency(links_frame([("A", "B"), ("B", "C")]), ["A", "B", "C"])     # (source, receiver)
    hop1, hop2 = propagate_shock(adjacency, np.array([1.0, 0.0, 0.0]))
    assert hop1.tolist() == [0.0, 0.5, 0.0] and hop2.tolist() == [0.0, 0.0, 0.25]

def test_incoming_weights_are_normalized():
    adjacency, _ = build_adjacency(links_frame([("A", "C"), ("B", "C")]), ["A", "B", "C"])
    hop1, _ = propagate_shock(adjacency, np.array([1.0, 0.0, 0.0]))
    assert hop1[2] == pytest.approx(0.25)                 # half of C's incoming weight, times alpha

def test_no_shock_returns_to_its_origin():
    adjacency, _ = build_adjacency(links_frame([("A", "B"), ("B", "A")]), ["A", "B"])
    _, hop2 = propagate_shock(adjacency, np.array([1.0, 0.0]))
    assert hop2[0] == 0.0

def test_unknown_nodes_and_isolated_receivers_are_counted():
    _, report = build_adjacency(links_frame([("A", "B"), ("ZZZ", "B")]), ["A", "B", "C"])
    assert (report.links_dropped_unknown_node, report.receivers_without_links) == (1, 2)
```
